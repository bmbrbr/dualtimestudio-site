Privacy

Effective November 3, 2026

This policy covers this website, dualtimestudio.com. Each of our apps has its own privacy policy, listed at the bottom of this page.

What this site collects

Nothing directly. This site has no accounts, no sign-in, no forms, no analytics, and no advertising. It doesn't set cookies or store anything in your browser.

Services this site relies on

  • Hosting. The site is served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). GitHub may log your IP address and request details for security and operations.
  • Fonts. Typefaces load from Google Fonts. Google may receive standard connection data such as your IP address.
  • DNS. The domain is managed through Cloudflare, Inc.

When you email us

If you write to hello@dualtimestudio.com, we use your email address and message only to reply to you. We don't add you to a mailing list or share your message with anyone. You can ask us to delete our correspondence at any time.

Changes

If this site starts collecting anything, we'll update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect.
